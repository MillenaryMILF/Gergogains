2. Digital content and the EU 14-day withdrawal right

As an EU consumer you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. For digital content delivered immediately there is a specific rule: you lose that right once delivery begins, but only if you expressly consented to immediate delivery and acknowledged that you thereby lose the right of withdrawal.

What this means in practice. The Plan ($19) is delivered instantly. For the waiver to be valid, your checkout must capture that express consent and acknowledgement — Lemon Squeezy offers a setting for this on digital products. If you do not enable it, customers keep the full 14-day right even after receiving and using the plan.

If something is technically broken — a file will not open, the plan did not generate — contact us and we will fix it or refund you in full.

  • After the programme is substantially delivered: no refund, but tell us what went wrong — we will try to make it right.
