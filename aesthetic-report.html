03

Action Plan

Skincare · 01

Protect & even the skin

Daily broad-spectrum SPF 30–50, especially on snow and at altitude where reflection doubles exposure. Add a simple gentle cleanser and a fragrance-free moisturiser. Expect visibly clearer, more even tone within weeks.

Highest leverage
Grooming · 02

Frame the jaw

Keep the beard short but defined — clean the neckline and cheek line every few days. A subtly lower, more natural hair tone (or grown-out roots) will read richer against fair skin than the brightest blonde.

Quick win
Style · 03

Dress the frame

The shoulder-to-waist ratio is an asset — lean into structured, fitted cuts (knit polos, tailored overshirts, mid-rise trousers) rather than oversized fits that hide it. Cooler, muted tones flatter the colouring.

Maintain
Presentation · 04

Photograph it well

Soft, slightly-above eye-level light and a relaxed half-smile suit your features far more than harsh overhead locker-room lighting. Prioritise rest and hydration before key photos to clear under-eye shadowing.
